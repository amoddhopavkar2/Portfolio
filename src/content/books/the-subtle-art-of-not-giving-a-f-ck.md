### My notes

- The desire for more positive experiences is itself a negative experience. Accepting negative experiences is itself a positive one.
- Not giving a f*ck about anything is still giving a f*ck about something. The question is what you choose to care about, and whether those are good values.
- Action isn't just the effect of motivation; it's also the cause of it.
- Good values are reality-based, socially constructive, immediate and controllable. Bad values are superstitious, socially destructive and out of your control.
- Not giving a f*ck doesn't mean being indifferent; it means being comfortable with being different.

### Values worth adopting

- Radical responsibility for everything that happens in your life.
- Uncertainty about your own beliefs.
- Failure: willingness to find your own flaws and mistakes.
- Rejection: the ability to both say no and hear no.
- Contemplating your own mortality.

### Questions to breed uncertainty

- What if I'm wrong?
- What would it mean if I were wrong?
- Would being wrong create a better or worse problem than my current one?

### Highlights

> The avoidance of suffering is a form of suffering.

> With great responsibility comes great power.

> Breadth of experience is likely necessary and desirable when you're young … But depth is where the gold is buried.
