### My notes

- The "new rich" follow DEAL: Definition, Elimination, Automation, Liberation.
- Your life can be measured by the number of awkward conversations you're willing to have.
- Stop being indecisive. When there's a deadlock, propose something and move forward.

### Highlights

> What we fear doing most is usually what we most need to do.

> Parkinson's Law dictates that a task will swell in (perceived) importance and complexity in relation to the time allotted for its completion.

> There are two types of mistakes: mistakes of ambition and mistakes of sloth.

> If you can't define it or act upon it, forget it.

> A good question to revisit whenever overwhelmed: Are you having a breakdown or a breakthrough?
