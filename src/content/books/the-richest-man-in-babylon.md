### My notes

- A part of all you earn is yours to keep.
- If you want to help a friend, do it in a way that doesn't bring their burdens onto you.
- Our "necessary expenses" will always grow to equal our income unless we push back.
- Better a little caution than a great regret.

### Seven cures for a lean purse

- Save at least a tenth of whatever you earn.
- Control your spending.
- Make your gold multiply.
- Guard your treasure from loss: invest only where the principal is safe.
- Make your home a profitable investment.
- Provide in advance for future income.
- Increase your ability to earn.

### Highlights

> Advice is one thing that is freely given away, but watch that you take only what is worth having.

> Opportunity is a haughty goddess who wastes no time with those who are unprepared.
