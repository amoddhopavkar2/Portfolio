### My notes

- The elephant in the brain: an important but unacknowledged feature of how our minds work; an introspective taboo.
- The social brain hypothesis: our ancestors got smart mainly to compete with each other socially and politically.
- The three big games our ancestors played: sex, social status and politics.
- "I have never let my schooling interfere with my education." — Mark Twain

### What we signal when we buy things

- A prosocial attitude: being a responsible citizen and good neighbor.
- Loyalty to a subculture: products as badges of membership.
- Being cool or in the know.
- Intelligence.

### What drives charitable giving

- Visibility: we give more when watched.
- Peer pressure.
- Proximity: we prefer to help locally.
- Relatability: faces and stories beat numbers and facts.
- Mating motive.
