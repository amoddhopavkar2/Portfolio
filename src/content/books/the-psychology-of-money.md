### My notes

- Financial success is not a hard science; it's a soft skill, where how you behave matters more than what you know.
- Your own experience with money is a tiny slice of what's happened in the world, but it shapes most of how you think the world works.
- No one is impressed with your possessions as much as you are.
- Good investing is not necessarily about making good decisions. It's about consistently not screwing up.
- Getting rich takes risk and optimism. Staying rich takes the opposite: humility, and fear that it can be taken away just as fast.
- The purpose of a margin of safety is to make the forecast unnecessary.
- The first rule of compounding is to never interrupt it unnecessarily.
- The hardest financial skill is getting the goalpost to stop moving. "Enough" is not too little.
- Be optimistic about the future but paranoid about what could stop you getting there.
- Plan on the plan not going according to plan.

### Rules I want to keep

- Less ego, more wealth.
- Manage money in a way that helps you sleep at night.
- Increase your time horizon.
- Use money to gain control over your time.
- Save. You don't need a specific reason.
- Worship room for error.
- Define the game you're playing.

### Highlights

> The highest form of wealth is the ability to wake up every morning and say, "I can do whatever I want today."

> Spending money to show people how much money you have is the fastest way to have less money.
