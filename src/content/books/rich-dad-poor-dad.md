### The six lessons

- The rich don't work for money.
- Why teach financial literacy?
- Mind your own business.
- The history of taxes and the power of corporations.
- The rich invent money.
- Work to learn. Don't work for money.

### My notes

- Broke is temporary; poor is eternal.
- An asset puts money in your pocket. A liability takes money out of it.
- "I can't afford it" shuts your brain down. "How can I afford it?" opens up possibilities.
- If you find you've dug yourself into a hole, stop digging.
- Keep your day job, but start buying real assets: businesses that don't need you, stocks, bonds, income-generating real estate, royalties.
- The rich buy luxuries last; the poor and middle class buy them first.
- Business owners earn, spend, then pay taxes. Employees earn, pay taxes, then spend.
- There is always risk, so learn to manage it instead of avoiding it.
- Know a little about a lot.
- Failure inspires winners. Failure defeats losers. Cynics criticize; winners analyze.
- Pay yourself first.
