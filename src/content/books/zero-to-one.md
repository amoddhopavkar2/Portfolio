### My notes

- Monopoly is the condition of every successful business.
- Every moment in business happens only once. The next Bill Gates won't build an operating system. If you're copying them, you aren't learning from them.
- To create and capture lasting value, don't build an undifferentiated commodity business.

### The dot-com lessons, and their opposites

- Make incremental advances → It is better to risk boldness than triviality.
- Stay lean and flexible → A bad plan is better than no plan.
- Improve on the competition → Competitive markets destroy profits.
- Focus on product, not sales → Sales matter just as much as product.

### What makes a monopoly

- Proprietary technology
- Network effects
- Economies of scale
- Branding
