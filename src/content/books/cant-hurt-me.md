### My notes

- The mind is like a governor on an engine: it caps you well below what you can actually do.
- Most people live to about 40% of their potential.
- Callous your mind: push against your mental limits at every opportunity.

### Highlights

> There is no finish line, Goggins. There is no finish line.

> If you want to master the mind and remove your governor, you'll have to become addicted to hard work.
