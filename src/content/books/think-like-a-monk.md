### My notes

- The cause of fear is attachment. The cure is detachment.
- Passion + expertise + usefulness = dharma.
- Spot a feeling, stop to understand it, swap in a new way of processing it.
- In getting you where you want to be, meditation may show you what you don't want to see.
- The four motivations: fear, desire, duty and love.

### Types of negative people

- Complainers complain endlessly.
- Cancellers take a compliment and spin it.
- Casualties think the world is against them.
- Critics judge others.
- Commanders push others to succeed past limits they accept for themselves.
- Competitors compare themselves with others.
- Controllers direct how their friends spend their time.
