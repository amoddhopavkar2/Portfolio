### Business

- Is the business simple and understandable?
- Does it have a consistent operating history?
- Does it have favorable long-term prospects?

### Management

- Is management rational?
- Is it candid with shareholders?
- Does it resist the institutional imperative?

### Financials

- Focus on return on equity, not earnings per share.
- Calculate owner earnings.
- Look for high profit margins.
- For every dollar retained, the company should create at least one dollar of market value.

### Market

- What is the business worth?
- Can it be bought at a significant discount to that value?

### The Buffett way

- Turn off the stock market.
- Don't worry about the economy.
- Buy a business, not a stock.
- Manage a portfolio of businesses.
